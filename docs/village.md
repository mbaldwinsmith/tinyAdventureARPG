# The clearing and Tessa’s General Store

The clearing keeps its cottage, travel chest, farm plot, pond and existing exits. The northeast plot is reserved for the general store. The workshop and waterside plots are marked for future residents.

1. Take the southwest forest trail and look for Tessa near the Whispering Forest trailhead. She waits in an open spot in the retained entrance room.
2. Find the brass key and open the brass gate. Return to Tessa, face her and choose **Invite Tessa to the village**.
3. Return to the clearing. The northeast plot now has a blue-roofed shop. Face its door to enter. Use the south door inside to return to the same plot.
4. Talk to Tessa and ask about supplies. Accept **Stock the shelves**, then bring three carried Wood. The clearing’s starter chest supplies enough for the quest. Delivery consumes the wood and pays twelve coins once.

The village journal shows the next step, available wood, and the three visible stages: **Reserved plots marked**, **General store open**, and **Market stocked**. Tessa serves at the counter and checks stock between noon and 14:00 in game time. Her interaction prompt follows her. The store remains usable during both routines.

## Prices

| Item       | Buy     | Sell    |
| ---------- | ------- | ------- |
| Wood       | 2 coins | 1 coin  |
| Iron sword | 8 coins | 4 coins |
| Shield     | 6 coins | 3 coins |

Set **Trade quantity** for wood. Equipment trades one instance at a time. Unequip plain gear before selling it. Enchanted gear and relics are outside this starter price table. Purchases of a sword or shield create equipment items. The permanent sword and shield tools come from the starter supplies.

## Saved village progress

The resident record owns recruitment and the general store service. The supplies conversation records a flag, and the quest records completion and its one-time reward. Quest progress before delivery follows the wood currently carried. Shop trade and quest delivery check the entire transaction before committing. A full pack, insufficient money, protected gear or an old command leaves items, coins and instance IDs as they were.
